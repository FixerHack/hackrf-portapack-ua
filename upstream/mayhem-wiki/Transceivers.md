The **Transceiver** category contains apps that can both transmit and receive — operating in half-duplex mode (one direction at a time).

## Apps

| App | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| [[Microphone-Transceiver\|Microphone Transceiver]] | Half-duplex voice transceiver supporting NFM, WFM, AM, USB, LSB, DSB-SC. Supports PTT and VOX (AUTO) activation, CTCSS tone squelch, Roger beep, and separate TX/RX frequency tuning. Originally developed by Furrtek in 2017. |
| [[KISS-TNC\|KISS TNC]] | KISS mode Terminal Node Controller for packet radio / APRS two-way communication over AX.25. |
