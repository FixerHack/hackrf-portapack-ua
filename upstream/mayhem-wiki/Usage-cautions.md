⚠️ The LNA/PA of HackRF is sensitive and can get damaged! ⚠️

# Prevention
## SMA port
- DO NOT swap the antenna while power is on.
- If you use an antenna that has an exposed metal part, DO NOT touch the antenna with you hands/body (Static electricity can damage the LNA, or it is possible that the spec change of it, like bouncing, while you touching it, could lead issues).
- DO NOT transmit without an antenna.

## Monitoring range
- You shouldn't receive near high power transmitters, even if it's not in the range that you are monitoring.

# Diagnostic
- Try to listen to local radio. Try it with AMP both ON and OFF. 
- Try to capture and replay a non-rolling-code. Try it with AMP both on and off. 

Check more details about [tx](https://github.com/portapack-mayhem/mayhem-firmware/wiki/preamplifier-ic-replacement#tx-problems) and [rx](https://github.com/portapack-mayhem/mayhem-firmware/wiki/preamplifier-ic-replacement#rx-problems) issues.
