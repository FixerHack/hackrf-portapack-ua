Dino Game is an endless runner inspired by the offline dinosaur game. The dino runs automatically and the player must dodge oncoming obstacles — cacti and flying pterodactyls. Speed increases over time. The game also features a difficulty selector before starting.

This is an [external app](External-apps-informations) (`.ppma` file on the SD card).

# Controls

* **Select button** or **Up button**: Jump. Tap once for a standard jump.
* **Down button**: Duck (crouch under low-flying obstacles).
* **Encoder knob**: Also triggers jump.

# Scoring

Score increases continuously while the dino is alive. A high score is tracked between sessions.
