Blackjack is the classic casino card game against an automated dealer. The goal is to reach a hand value as close to 21 as possible without exceeding it. Face cards count as 10, Aces as 1 or 11. The dealer draws until reaching at least 17.

This is an [external app](External-apps-informations) (`.ppma` file on the SD card).

# Controls

* **Encoder knob** / **Left / Right buttons**: Navigate between Hit and Stand options.
* **Select button**: Confirm the chosen action.

# Gameplay

At the start of each round two cards are dealt to the player and two to the dealer, with one dealer card face-down. The player can:

* **Hit** — draw another card
* **Stand** — end their turn and let the dealer play

The dealer then reveals the hidden card and draws until reaching 17 or higher. The higher hand without busting wins. A high score is saved persistently.
