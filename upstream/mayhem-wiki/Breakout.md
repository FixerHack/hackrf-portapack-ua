# Controls

- 'select' to start the game
- 'select' to launch the ball
- 'left' and 'right' keys or rotary encoder to move the paddle
- Simultaneously press key 'left' and key 'up' => put the focus on the hidden 'go back to main menu' icon on the top right part of the screen, click 'select' key to exit the game or optionally tap the top left corner of screen to exit.
