## Colors

Each application icon has a color indicating its implementation status:

* Green — mostly fully implemented.
* Yellow — working, but some features are missing.
* Orange — beta / in development, may not work reliably.
* Red — destructive utility (e.g. Wipe SD Card).

## External Apps

External apps (`.ppma` files) are placed in the `APPS/` folder on the SD card. Their version must match the currently running firmware. Developers may move lesser-used or incomplete apps from internal flash to external to free up space for new applications
